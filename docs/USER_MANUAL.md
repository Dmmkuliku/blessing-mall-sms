# Blessing Mall SMS — User Manual

## Roles

| Role | Main duties |
|------|-------------|
| Cashier | Point of Sale, view inventory |
| Store Manager | Inventory, purchases, suppliers, expenses, reports, view staff |
| Store Owner | Everything, including staff accounts and shop settings |

## Sign in

1. Start the system from the Desktop shortcut.
2. Choose your role.
3. Enter work email and password.
4. Click **Sign in securely**.

## Point of Sale

1. Open **Point of Sale**.
2. Search by name, SKU, or barcode.
3. Add items to the cart and adjust quantities.
4. Choose payment method (Cash, M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, Card, Bank).
5. Enter amount paid and complete the sale.
6. Print the receipt if needed.

## Inventory

- View stock levels and reorder alerts.
- Managers/owners can add or edit products.
- Cashiers can view products but do not see supplier cost prices.

## Purchases

- Record goods received from suppliers.
- Stock quantities increase automatically when a purchase is posted.

## Expenses & Reports

- Record operating costs (rent, utilities, transport, and others).
- Use Reports for sales summaries, product performance, and cash flow.

## Good practice

- Do not share passwords.
- Sign out at the end of a shift.
- Keep the Start window open while the shop is trading.
- Back up `data\blessing-mall.db` every day.
